The AI guide

a design system machines can read

A design system starts to drift the moment a machine starts drawing. So we wrote it down as a generation contract: a skill an AI agent loads before it touches a screen.

Reuse before inventing, one truth source, and a list of things it must never do.

  • No painted-on cyan

    The brand lives in the page’s wash; it goes flat the moment a second card does it.

  • No fake premium

    No neon shadows, no mystery blobs, no generic purple AI styling.

  • One layer of glass

    The outer bento is glass, the content inside is solid: never glass on glass.

  • One action surface

    Zero or one saturated action card. Two means the page lost its anchor.

  • No border scaffolding

    Structure comes from tone and spacing, not boxes drawn around boxes.

  • No heavy type

    Hierarchy by size, spacing and colour, never bold body copy.

and fail the output if text clips, colours don’t explain state, card shadows form square artifacts, cards nest, or you can’t tell the next action
