PantryPal

an AI cooking partner

PantryPal turns the ingredients you already have into meals you actually want to cook. Snap your fridge or your receipt — computer vision stocks your digital shelf, and an AI recipe engine does the rest.

Designed and built end-to-end: research, wireframes, a six-screen hi-fi prototype, and working code with a live Gemini API integration.

02

Problem

good food, wasted

Thrown away, uneaten

The USDA estimates 35% of the U.S. food supply is wasted every year — often just because no one knows what to make with what's left.

Recipes without a kitchen

The internet has millions of recipes — and almost none of them start from what's actually sitting on your shelf.

built for first-apartment cooks college students busy families

which raises the question

How might we make dinner start from what's already on the shelf?

03

Solution

six screens, zero friction

log in

A warm front door — “Let's get cooking!” sets the tone before a single tap.

ingredients hub

One screen, three jobs: view, add, and select ingredients — no detours, no repeats.

ai scanner

Point the camera at a fridge or a receipt; computer vision stocks the shelf for you.

checkout

A quick pass over the picks, then one big button: find recipes.

recipes

Generated meals with nutrition at a glance — choose whatever looks good tonight.

cook

04

Process

how the shelf won

01 · bucketsset aside

Fridge / freezer / pantry categories sounded tidy — and cost extra clicks at every turn.

02 · the portalset aside

A separate add-then-select portal showed the same screen twice with slightly different jobs.

03 · one shelfshipped

Add, view, and choose on a single screen, organized by food type instead of storage. Friction gone — it became the six screens you just walked.

what I learned

Own the whole arc — blank canvas → visual language → UX → working code with a live AI API.

Test early, with real people — peer feedback surfaced blind spots no solo pass would catch.

Ship systems, not artifacts — PantryPal is built to keep evolving well past v1.

What I'd do next — test with cooks I've never met; friendly feedback only gets you so far.
