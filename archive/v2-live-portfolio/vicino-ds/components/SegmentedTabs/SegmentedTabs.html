SegmentedTabs

Overview source for Pulse segmented controls. Each animated tab group is paired with its own frozen state slices below, and the placeable variants also live in their own folders for direct preview and later extraction.

Two roles, two looks. The white-thumb family (PageNavTabs / base SegmentedTabs) is the section / view switcher — moving between top-level views of a surface. Give it page-level weight: pair it with a section title (title left, switcher right). The ink-thumb family (ReportRangeTabs / ActionRangeTabs, .segmented-thumb { background: var(--ink-900) }) is the compact in-group range / mode toggle — it sits inline above the thing it filters and never gets a section title. Don't use one look for both, or the user can't tell a view switch from a small filter.

Panel behavior: on tab change the thumb slides under the active label and the associated panel content crossfades as one block — fade the panel out, swap content while it is hidden, then fade it back in (~300ms total). Do not fade rows individually; per-row fades read as a blink. Honor prefers-reduced-motion by swapping instantly.

Behavior / OverflowTabs

Overflow — wrap, never clip

When a group is wider than its container it wraps to a second row instead of scrolling off-canvas behind a hidden scrollbar. The thumb is measured on both axes, so it follows the active tab onto the wrapped row. Give the group room on wide layouts; only tight columns wrap.
