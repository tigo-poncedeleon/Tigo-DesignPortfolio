ApprovalChain

Sequential approval timeline used wherever Pulse routes a decision through people: the campaign plan gate (Gate A), per-post brief and content approval (Gate B①/B②), and the inbox review drawer. One renderer drives them all.

Identity vs. meaning. The avatar carries who and is intentionally neutral (initials on a tonal fill) so it never competes with status colour. Each step's state lives in a small badge clipped to the avatar corner and in the status label: green = approved, cyan = active / your turn, gray = waiting, amber = changes requested. The active step also gets a cyan ring. Changes-requested is amber, never red — red is reserved for declining data.

Rail fills with progress. The connector below an approved step turns green (--success-200), so how far the chain has advanced reads at a glance.

Motion. On mount, steps stagger in (opacity + 6px rise, 55ms apart) and badges pop (scale). The rail and active ring transition on state change. All of it is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
