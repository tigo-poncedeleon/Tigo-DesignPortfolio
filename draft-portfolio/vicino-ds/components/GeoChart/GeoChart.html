World choropleth for audience-by-region. Regions are shaded by the one-hue sequential cyan ramp, class-binned (≥40% / 20–39% / 10–19% / <10%); no-data landmass stays neutral. The scale legend names the bins and the ranked list carries exact values + MoM deltas — the map is never the only way to read them. Hover or focus a region (or its row) for the read-out. Region shapes: Robinson-projected Natural Earth 110m (public domain), also available as assets/maps/world-regions.svg.
