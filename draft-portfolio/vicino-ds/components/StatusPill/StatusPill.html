StatusPill

Status labels come in two outlined sizes — default for cards, heros, and popovers, and compact for dense rows (e.g. the production-queue list). Both share one shape and color system: green means positive, active/live, or approved; blue means suggested action or scheduled; amber means risk, negative, or on hold; cyan means ready; purple means in progress. Red is reserved for data-decline only.
