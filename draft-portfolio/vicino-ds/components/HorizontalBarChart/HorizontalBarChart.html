HorizontalBarChart

Horizontal bar scaffold for rankings, share, and meters — Pulse's most reused analytics pattern, with a distinct form per job so the variants never blur together: ranked rows (rank digits, thick bars, ink leader), icon share meters (hatched track + trend pills), ONE stacked share strip with keyed region rows, score tiles where the value leads, and a card panel with a CTA footer. Bar length carries the ranking; the ink leader is the black anchor, never rank coloring. The hatched track appears only when the value is a true fraction of a whole (share, score out of 100) — open-scale comparisons stay on bare bars.
