01

In conversation

About told as a chat, in Home’s own bubbles. It plays by itself when it comes into view: two questions, the photo, two answers. The chips at the foot ask the rest.

What’s new
No page of prose at all. The site opens as a conversation, and About carries it on.
Why it’s nice
The most “you” of the eight: it has your voice, and it shows the chat before anyone types into it.
Watch for
It is scripted, not the live model. The chips could hand a question to Home’s real composer.

02

A sentence with pictures

One paragraph at the frame’s one size, with small pastel tiles set into the words where emoji would go. The photo is one of them, and it opens when you point at it.

What’s new
All words, and still full of colour: the menu’s pastels come back, one per idea.
Why it’s nice
Reads in one breath. It keeps the frame’s single size and needs no layout at all.
Watch for
The photo is small until pointed at, so on a phone it needs a tap. More tiles than this gets busy.

04

The badge

The sentences on the left, and on the right a name badge on a lanyard, hanging from the top of the window. Drag it and it swings on a spring; click it and it turns over. The back says how to reach you.

What’s new
A physical object with a front and a back: who you are, then how to find you.
Why it’s nice
Playful the way Play is, and the springs the site runs on finally get something to hold.
Watch for
A gimmick if pushed further. The photo is smaller than in the tile options.
